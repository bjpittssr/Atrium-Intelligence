# Build Inspiration Notes

VisionDeck was designed around a simple operating thesis:

> See more. Know first. Act with precision.

BalanceMi turns pressure into visible structure: health, maintenance, family,
finance, scheduling, learning, and future model support. The public surface is
intentionally restrained: polished enough to feel premium, practical enough to
stay useful, and bounded enough to avoid pretending the UI is the system
authority.
